## Welcome to the Platform

If you just created your account and you're wondering where to begin, you're in the right place. This guide covers every way you can earn on the platform, how much you can realistically make, and the best strategy to get started fast.

## What Can You Earn Here?

The platform offers multiple income streams so you're never dependent on just one. Here's a quick overview:

- **Mining** — Earn passively just by keeping the platform open
- **Short Links** — Share links and earn every time someone visits
- **Referrals** — Invite friends and earn a percentage of their earnings forever
- **Games** — Play Rock Paper Scissors and win bonus rewards
- **YouTube Shorts** — Watch short videos and collect rewards
- **Courses** — Complete learning modules and earn upon completion

## Step 1: Complete Your Profile

Before you start earning, make sure your profile is 100% complete. A complete profile unlocks higher earning limits and builds trust on the platform.

## Step 2: Activate Mining

Mining is the easiest place to start because it runs in the background. Once activated, you earn continuously without doing anything. Think of it as your baseline income that runs 24/7.

Head to the **Mining** section and hit activate. That's it.

## Step 3: Share Your First Short Link

Short links are one of the fastest ways to earn. Take any URL, shorten it through the platform, and share it on social media, WhatsApp, or anywhere you have an audience. Every visit earns you money.

## Step 4: Refer Someone Today

Your referral link is the most powerful earning tool on the platform. Every person you refer earns you a percentage of everything they ever earn — for life. Even referring one active person makes a significant difference over time.

## Realistic Expectations

Don't expect to get rich overnight. Users who are consistent and combine multiple earning methods see the best results. The ones who do best treat it like a second income stream that grows steadily rather than a get-rich-quick scheme.

## Ready to Go?

Log in, activate your mining, share your first link, and send your referral link to at least three people. That's the best possible start you can make on day one.
