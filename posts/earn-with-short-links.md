## What Are Short Links?

Short links are monetized URLs. You take any web address, run it through our shortener, and share the result. Every time someone clicks your link and passes through the gateway page, you earn a reward.

It's the same as sharing a normal link — except this time you get paid for it.

## How to Create Your First Short Link

1. Go to the **Short Links** section in your dashboard
2. Paste any URL into the input field — a news article, a YouTube video, a useful tool, anything
3. Click shorten
4. Copy the new link and share it anywhere

Every click on that link earns you money. There's no limit to how many links you can create.

## What Links Work Best?

The best short links are ones people actually want to click. High-performing content types include:

- **News articles** — Trending stories get enormous click-through rates
- **Useful tools** — Free tools, calculators, converters, anything people search for
- **Entertainment** — Funny videos, interesting facts, viral content
- **Tutorials** — How-to guides in any niche

Avoid sharing random or spammy URLs — people won't click them and your reputation on social platforms will suffer.

## Where to Share Your Links

**WhatsApp and Telegram** — Group chats are goldmines if you're in active communities. Sharing one genuinely useful link a day keeps you visible without being annoying.

**Facebook groups** — Niche groups (cooking, gaming, finance, health) have engaged audiences. Share links that are relevant to the group topic.

**Twitter / X** — Tweet useful links with a short comment explaining why it's worth reading. Threads perform especially well.

**Forums and Reddit** — Only share in contexts where the link genuinely adds value. Communities are quick to spot spam and will ban you fast.

**Your own blog or website** — If you have any content platform, embedding monetized links naturally is the cleanest long-term strategy.

## How to Scale Your Short Link Earnings

The math is simple: more clicks = more earnings. To scale up:

- Share more frequently across more platforms
- Focus on evergreen content that gets clicks for weeks, not just days
- Build an audience that trusts your link recommendations
- Track which links perform best and create more content in those niches

## Combining Short Links With Referrals

When you refer someone who actively uses short links, you earn from their clicks too. This creates a compounding effect — your network's activity earns you money even when you're not actively sharing anything yourself.

## Getting Started Today

Pick three links right now — a news article you found interesting, a useful tool you use, and a piece of entertainment. Shorten all three, share them to wherever your audience is, and watch your first short link earnings come in.

The habit of turning every share into a monetized share is the single easiest change you can make to increase your earnings immediately.
