## Why Referrals Are the Best Earning Method

Every other earning method on the platform requires your time — watching videos, clicking links, playing games. Referrals are different. Once someone signs up through your link, you earn a cut of everything they earn, forever, without doing anything extra.

That's what makes referrals the only truly passive income stream on the platform.

## How the Referral System Works

When someone signs up using your referral link:

1. They get credited to your account as a referral
2. Every time they earn — through mining, short links, games, or anything else — you receive a percentage
3. This continues indefinitely, even years later

The more active your referrals are, the more you earn. So the goal isn't just to refer as many people as possible — it's to refer people who will actually use the platform.

## Where to Find Your Referral Link

Go to the **Referral** section in your dashboard. You'll find your unique link there. Copy it and share it anywhere — social media, messaging apps, forums, wherever your audience is.

## Who Should You Refer?

The best referrals are people who are genuinely looking for ways to earn online. Think about:

- Friends and family who complain about needing extra income
- Communities online focused on side hustles and passive income
- Students looking for flexible ways to make money
- Stay-at-home parents looking for something they can do from their phone

Referring people who aren't interested is a waste of your time. Quality matters more than quantity.

## Strategies That Actually Work

**Content creation** — If you make YouTube videos, TikToks, or write blog posts about earning online, embed your referral link in the description. This brings in referrals on autopilot.

**WhatsApp and Telegram groups** — Share your experience and results in earning-focused groups. A genuine success story is far more persuasive than a cold link drop.

**Your existing social following** — Even a small following can generate referrals if the message is right. Share your dashboard results and let the numbers do the talking.

## Tracking Your Referrals

The referral dashboard shows you how many people you've referred, how active they are, and how much you've earned from them. Check it regularly to see which channels are bringing in the most active users.

## The Long Game

One highly active referral can earn you more over a year than hundreds of passive ones. Focus on bringing in people who will actually engage with the platform, help them get started, and your referral income will compound over time.
